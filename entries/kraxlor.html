[WIP] The alias under which I will be sharing future turntablism content, using portable and half-DIY gear.

Why half-DIY?


Long story short, I got this SC500 from PortablismGear, instead of assembling one myself. I foresaw a couple of advantages in doing so, hence the pragmatism. In reality, the unit alone is good enough for a hobbyist/novice, however one can always improve the setup in other ways—the DIY part.

Specifically:

  • Peli 1040 Micro Case (black)
  • Powerbank (5k)
  • Velcro Cable Ties
  • USB-C to 3.5mm Cable (black)
  • USB-A to USB-C Manhattan Coiled Charging Cable (black)
  • SanDisk Ultra Fit USB 3.2 Flash Drive(s) (64Gb)
  • (more to come)

The goal here is to have a portable workstation for comfortable sofa and outdoor sessions alike.
