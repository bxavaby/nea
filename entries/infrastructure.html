Infrastructure


In this page, I note my current infrastructure, justify it, and suggest alternatives based on my preferences.

Mobile

Rather a careless choice, but all my 3 phones are Apple devices. As of today's date, I use only the iPhone 16 Pro, and keep both the iPhone 11 and iPhone 11 Pro in inventory, may necessity strike. When it comes to phones, I value convenience and feel over mostly anything else. Privacy is stupidly important to me, don't get me wrong... I just don't care enough to leave the ecosystem, buy a Pixel and install GrapheneOS on it, which is still the digital nomad's safest bet. On the flip side, I select software carefully, as per my usual privacy standards.

Software (gotta improve)

Now comes the voice you've been waiting for, AKA the political stuff. All in all (SPOILER ALERT), an average privacy stack comprised of easy decisions, if one cares to make them.

  • OS: dual-booting Arch and NixOS on my main machine; dying to try out Void!
  • Email: started with Tuta Mail and Mailfence, now on Proton Mail; gotta say, it is wonderful, but I fear not for long...
  • VPN: tried both Mullvad VPN and Proton VPN — the first is great, however a bit less performant for my setup; do not recommend using the latter alongside their email client, beware.
  • Browser: Tor when needed, Brave usually; would still suggest a hardened Firefox or simply LibreWolf.
  • Password Manager: Bitwarden, because trying to move away from the Proton ecosystem; recommend Proton Pass if you don't use anything else from them.
  • Authenticator: Ente Auth, same reason and suggestion as above when it comes to Proton Authenticator.
  • Cloud: keep rockin Proton Drive, but recommend you just use NextCloud (RIP me ATP); I am a little lazy, you know...
  • File Sharing: got OnionShare just for show, because I always use my custom script beam.sh — more here.
  • Git: use both GitHub and Codeberg; truthfully, the second is superior, but not mature yet; GitHub is getting scary, man...

Additional goods:

  • DNS: Quad9's nice
  • Metadata: ExifTool
  • Payment: disposable cards for the most part, Monero where possible
  • Hosting: for I feel shame, will simply suggest Njalla and Bitlaunch — pay with crypto.
  • Remote Access: Tailscale works great and pairs well with Termius (not much experience here)

Will leave it at that for now. B-bird!
